Hi, I'm Tsucak.

I'm a synth enthusiast, not a programmer. I build VCV Rack modules and related tools. The ideas and concepts are all mine; I work them out and bring them to life together with an AI (Claude), which writes most of the code. I make the design decisions and test the results.

**How I try to keep it honest**

- I don't take "it compiles" as proof it works. I check things by listening and measuring in Rack.
- I stop between bigger steps and make sure I can explain what each part does.
- The READMEs and notes are a record of the work. We have nothing to hide.

**What I'm working on**

- **Tsucak Bridge** (coming soon): a general-purpose local HTTP bridge for VCV Rack. Anything that can talk HTTP can inspect and control a running patch: scripts, other apps, or an AI assistant through MCP. It listens on localhost only, requires a token, and write access is off by default.
- **Drone module** (in design): a self-oscillating tone generator inspired by the Moog ladder filter, built for stable, controllable drones.

Feedback and questions are welcome.
